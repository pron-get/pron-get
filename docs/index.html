pron-get v0.7.2 . apt-get for porn, over tor

*** new! pron-get 0.7.0 is out . now with sources.list, update and upgrade . every request goes through tor . resumes downloads . parallel downloads with -j . has super cow powers ***

remember porn-get? in 2001 someone wrote a bash script that made downloading porn feel like apt-get. it used lynx and wget and it really worked. pron-get is the same idea rebuilt in go for 2026: same verbs, same jokes, but it talks to the site through tor so the site never sees your ip.

what is it new!

$ pron-get search oil massage
$ pron-get install -q 720 <viewkey>
$ pron-get add-source https://www.pornhub.com/model/example-model
$ pron-get update && pron-get upgrade -j 3
$ pron-get moo
  • works like apt: search, install, remove, update, upgrade, list
  • tor or nothing: no request leaves without a confirmed tor exit, no dns outside tor
  • a fresh tor circuit for every video, so downloads can't be tied together
  • interrupted downloads resume where they stopped
  • follows models, channels, playlists and searches through a sources.list
  • pron-cache searches what you've indexed, offline

q: is this for real?
a: yes. it's a joke that actually works, which was the whole point in 2001 too.

q: what does it download?
a: public videos only. premium, private and locked videos, geo-blocks and age checks get reported, never bypassed.

q: does tor make me invisible?
a: to the site, mostly. your own disk still has the files on it.
